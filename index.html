CAJM Practice

CAJM Practice is a private office application. One accounting practice uses it on its own Mac to keep client work, documents and email in one place. It is not offered to the public, and nobody can sign up for it.

Gmail access

The owner connects the practice's own mailboxes with Google's read-only Gmail permission. The app reads mail. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes mail.
