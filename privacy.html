Privacy policy — CAJM Practice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who runs the app

CAJM Practice is a private application. Its owner runs it for one accounting practice. Contact: cajmkurian@gmail.com.

Google data the app uses

The app uses only Gmail messages from mailboxes that the owner connects. It uses Google's read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly). It does not ask for permission to send, delete or change mail.

How the data is used

The app shows the practice's email to the owner, helps the owner file each email to the right client, and prepares work for the owner's review. The app never sends email.

Where the data is stored

Email is stored encrypted on the owner's Mac. Encrypted backup copies are kept in the practice's own Google Drive.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with other companies and not used for advertising. Today, no email content is sent to any AI service. If that changes, this page will be updated first and will name the service.

Limited Use

CAJM Practice's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A mailbox owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. For questions about stored email, write to cajmkurian@gmail.com.
